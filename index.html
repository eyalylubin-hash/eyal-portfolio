Research & data analysis

Alcohol Consumption & DUI Fatalities

Research project · 2025

I used R to compare alcohol consumption and fatalities involving impaired drivers across U.S. states. I found a positive association between beer consumption and DUI fatalities, then examined how the relationship differed for other types of alcohol.

Education & Entrepreneurship

Research project · 2025

I analyzed Global Entrepreneurship Monitor data to ask whether education and personal networks are related to how easy people think it is to start a business. Across countries, average education had almost no correlation with perceived ease (r = 0.012).
